        From order to doorstep

        Four steps, two to three days. Nothing is baked until the first one happens, so the date you pick is the date you get.

        1. Order placed

          Day 0

          Pick a catalogue box or build your own, choose a date at least two days out, and send it through.

        2. Confirmed & paid

          Day 0–1

          She confirms availability for your date and takes an advance to lock in the slot.

        3. She bakes

          Day 1–2

          Everything is made fresh in the one to two days before your date — nothing sits on a shelf waiting for a buyer.

        4. Pickup or delivery

          Day 2–3

          Collect from Mohini Park or get it delivered, right on your chosen date.

        Before you order

        Still unsure about something? Message her directly.

        orders@example.com
        How much notice do you need?

        Most cakes, hampers and cookie boxes need 2–3 days' notice. The order date is blocked out for anything sooner than that.

        Can I fully customise a hamper?

        Yes — pick a box size and fill it item by item, or ask for a fully custom size for larger gifting and corporate orders.

        Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

        Delivery area and charges to be confirmed before your order is locked in.

        How do I pay?

        She'll confirm the payment method and advance amount when you book your date.

        What about allergies or dietary needs?

        Tell her when you order and she'll let you know what she can adjust.

        Can I cancel or change my order?

        Cancellation and change policy to be confirmed before your order is locked in.
